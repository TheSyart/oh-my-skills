# Report Writing

The report should be direct, specific, and evidence-backed. The target reader is an ordinary viewer deciding whether the video is worth their time.

## Voice

- Low scores require sharper language, but every sharp claim must tie back to a timecode, subtitle, screenshot, or claim-review row.
- High scores should not be forced into sarcasm; explain what is useful and where boundaries remain.
- Critique content quality, evidence, claims, and sales logic. Do not attack the creator personally.

## Avoid Soft Filler

Do not use vague softeners like:

- "有一定启发"
- "可以作为参考"
- "总体还可以"
- "可能对部分用户有帮助"

Only use these if immediately followed by a concrete limitation.

## Must Include

- A first-screen verdict and one-sentence reason.
- Six scores with short evidence-based rationale.
- Anxiety marketing judgment.
- Empty-content judgment.
- Claim review table.
- Screenshot and subtitle evidence cards.
- Corrected transcript summary, not a full raw transcript dump.

## High-Risk Domains

For medical, legal, financial, psychological, education, or career decisions, add a short risk note and verify claims with high-quality sources when browsing is allowed.
