# Evidence Selection

Choose 3-6 timepoints. Each evidence point must connect a screenshot to corrected subtitle text and a concrete evaluation reason.

## Priority Timepoints

- Opening promise: what the video says it will deliver.
- Fear hook: the clearest anxiety, scarcity, shame, or elimination claim.
- Key claim: a definition, causal claim, number, tool recommendation, or method.
- Proof gap: where a large claim appears without a source, demo, example, or reasoning.
- Value moment: any genuinely useful explanation or step.
- Conversion action: private message, paid course, group, training camp, data pack, or follow/like bait.

## Time Calculation

Use the midpoint of the subtitle sentence when possible:

```text
seconds = (begin_time + end_time) / 2000
```

## Rules

- Capture full frames; do not crop vertical or horizontal videos.
- Keep original black bars or platform framing if present.
- Do not use screenshots alone as proof. Always quote the corrected subtitle fragment.
- If a video has no useful visual proof, still capture representative frames and say what the frame fails to substantiate.
