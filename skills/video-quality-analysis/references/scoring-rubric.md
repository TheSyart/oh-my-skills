# Scoring Rubric

Each dimension is 0-5. Higher is better. Total score is 30.

## Dimensions

- Knowledge value: useful knowledge, transferable framing, real insight, or concrete explanation.
- Concept accuracy: definitions, cause-effect relationships, boundaries, and risk statements are correct.
- Evidence quality: sources, cases, data, screenshots, demos, or reasoning chains support the claims.
- Information density: high ratio of useful information to repetition, slogans, filler, and generic hype.
- Anxiety manipulation: high score means little fear selling; low score means fear, shame, scarcity, or FOMO is central.
- Actionability: advice is concrete, executable, bounded, and clear about prerequisites.

## Per-Dimension Anchors

- 5: strong, specific, verifiable, and useful.
- 3: partly useful but shallow, underspecified, or weakly evidenced.
- 1: mostly empty, exaggerated, unsupported, or misleading.
- 0: no meaningful value or actively harmful guidance.

## Recommendation Thresholds

- Recommended: total `23+`, with no severe concept error or strong anxiety funnel.
- Caution: total `14-22`, or useful fragments mixed with weak evidence, oversimplification, or low information density.
- Not recommended: total `13-`, or severe concept error, obvious anxiety marketing, conversion bait, or mostly empty slogans.

If a threshold and a severe issue conflict, keep the score-based label but explain the severe issue in the verdict.
